## What the FruitSeg result means

FruitSeg30 is a cross-dataset, fruit-only test. The new model scores mask mAP50 17.06% and pixel F1 4.19% for Fruits, while predicting many non-fruit pixels as Staple Food. This is a genuine domain-shift failure, not a typo. Report it as a limitation and as evidence for the generalization/failure-analysis contribution. Do not repeat the old 'Fruits is the best class' claim without clarifying that it refers to internal validation.
